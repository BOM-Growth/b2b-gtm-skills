# Cohort taxonomy

Read when picking the week's cohort. Order within the table is the default order to run them; results change the order.

| Cohort | Who | Angle | Cheap to fail? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cold, small accounts, practitioner persona | Tier 3 inside the gates, the user persona | An offer sized for them; a way to try it on their own data | Yes | Tests the message; low cost if it flops |
| Cold, mid accounts, manager persona | Tier 2, the manager | A peer's result, in their terms | Yes | Same message family, next rung |
| Cold, named accounts, executive persona | Tier 1, owner or executive | Executive to executive; the business result; phone calls | No | Only after the lower rungs produced replies |
| Warm, past prospects | Demoed or talked more than a year ago, never bought | What's new since you looked | Yes | Different tack from cold: assume they know us |
| Closed-lost by reason: missing feature | Lost on a gap now closed | "You said X was missing; here it is" | Yes | Sales calls them first |
| Closed-lost by reason: too small or budget | Lost on size or price | New package or pricing | Yes | Watch for accounts that grew |
| Closed-lost by reason: chose a competitor | Lost to a named competitor | Only where the change beats them on the stated reason | No | Otherwise wait until their contract is up for review |
| Event follow-up | Scans and meetings from a show | What they saw and asked at the event | Yes | Runs through event-follow-up with its SLA |
| Signal-driven | New followers, site visitors, mentions on calls | The thing they looked at | Yes | Small, continuous, through signal-to-outreach |
| Customer expansion | Customers below their user potential | The next team or the next workflow | Yes | Owned with success |

## Persona ladder

Run a cohort in rungs a day or two apart: practitioner, then manager, then executive. Executive rungs carry phone touches. Stop a rung when replies show the message is wrong rather than pushing the next rung with it.

## Sequence shape

Default six touches over 21 days by email, plus calls on the manager and executive rungs. `context/stack.md` names the tool; `context/funnel.md` names what counts as a result (a booked demo creates the deal).
