# Customer interview question bank

Read when building the core ten for an interview. Pick by angle; keep questions open.

## The core ten (default)

1. Walk me through how this part of your work was done before.
2. What was the moment you decided something had to change?
3. What did you look at, and what made you choose this?
4. What did the first week actually look like?
5. Who on your team uses it, and how did they take to it?
6. Tell me about one project where it mattered. What happened?
7. What numbers do you watch, and what did they do? (Let them give the number and the caveat.)
8. What surprised you?
9. What would you tell a peer who is where you were a year ago?
10. What do you want it to do next?

## Bank by theme

| Theme | Questions |
|---|---|
| Before | What took the longest? What did you dread? Who did the work and how many of them? |
| Decision | Who had to agree? What almost stopped it? What did the skeptic say? |
| Adoption | What was hard at first? What made it click? What did your most experienced person think? |
| Results | What can you do now that you could not before? How many more of X? What did that mean for the business? |
| People | Did anyone's job change? What do they do with the time? |
| Advice | What would you do differently? What should we do differently? |

## Call rules

- Record with consent; say when recording starts.
- Silence is the tool; wait after a number.
- Numbers in their words, with their caveat; do not round up for them.
- Ask who else should speak.
- No pitching, no roadmap.
- Thank them and tell them what happens next and when.
