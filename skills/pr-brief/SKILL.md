This applies when a release, a customer result, a hire, a funding event or a data point could carry a story. It produces the one-page brief a PR partner or the founder can act on.

## Before you start

This skill works on its own. If the person has their own marketing brain (or any folder of context files about their company), read the files listed under `reads` in the header first and cite them for every fact. If they do not have one, do not look for one: ask for the two or three facts this play needs and go on. Never invent a fact; a blank is a question for the person.

Tools: this play speaks in verbs (check the CRM, pull the call, schedule the post). `TOOLS.md` at the root of this repo lists the tools recommended for each verb. The company's own `1-context/stack.md` wins when it exists. No tool is required; a spreadsheet and a mail client cover most of it.

## The play

1. Read the context (see Before you start). Name the trigger (release, customer milestone, data, hire, funding, event) and the date.
2. Find the angle: what changes for the reader, not what the company did. A journalist runs the industry story the company's news proves; test it against the outlets' last ten headlines.
3. Pull proof through `permission-check`: the customer who will speak, the number that can be published, the screenshot that can be shown. A story with no publishable proof is a blog post, not PR.
4. Choose the spokesperson and draft their three quotes in `founder-voice` or the exec's voice; the customer's quote through the proof tracker.
5. Target outlets and reporters by beat, from the trade-press list in `1-context/stack.md` or built fresh: who covered the competitor, who covered the category, who covered the customer's industry.
6. Set the embargo, the exclusive offer if any, and the timing against `5-current/calendar.md` (never into a known dead zone or against a bigger industry story).
7. Assets: headshot, logo, product screenshots cleared for press, a one-paragraph boilerplate from `brand-voice`.
8. Hand to `press-release` for the release and `trade-press-placement` for the pitch sequence. Draft only; the human sends.

## What good looks like

- The best operator finds the industry story first and places the company inside it.
- The mediocre version writes "Company announces feature" and sends it to a wire.
- Good output is a brief a reporter could turn into a story without a follow-up call.

## Rules

- MUST clear every customer, number and screenshot through `permission-check` before it enters the brief.
- MUST check the timing against `5-current/calendar.md`.
- NEVER send a pitch or a release; the human sends.
- NEVER promise an exclusive to more than one outlet.
