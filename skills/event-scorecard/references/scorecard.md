# Event scorecard rubric

Read when scoring a show. This rubric has no built-in numbers. Set the lead-time minimum, the fail threshold, the payback ratio and the payback horizon in `5-current/calendar.md` before scoring, and ask the person for any that are missing.

## Gate

| Check | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Lead time | More than the minimum you set in `5-current/calendar.md` | Under it: pass on the show, calendar next year's |

## Eight tests

| Test | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Industry fit | The audience is the ICP's industry | Adjacent or general |
| Segment depth | Our segment has its own track or a large share of attendees | Our segment is a small part of a broad show |
| Audience composition | Buyer roles outnumber vendors on the floor | Vendors, media and students dominate |
| Past performance | A prior year produced SQLs and ARR against the payback test | No prior data, or prior data missed |
| Speaking and sponsorship | A session or sponsorship is available and the deadline is open | Both closed or not offered |
| Parallel programming | Sessions do not empty the floor during booth hours | Floor hours compete with the main program |
| Partner economics | A partner shares a booth, a dinner or a list | No partner angle |
| Competitive presence | Competitors present, so prospects are comparing on site | Competitors absent and the category is not on the agenda |

Fails at or above your threshold means pass on the show.

## Payback

Fully loaded cost = booth + sponsorship + travel + people days at loaded rate + collateral + shipping.

Target = cost x your payback ratio, in new ARR within your payback horizon. Set both before scoring.

Deals implied = target / average deal size. If the implied deals exceed what the booth team can realistically book and close from the show's SQL history, the verdict is attend or pass.

## Formats

| Format | When |
|---|---|
| Exhibit | Passes the tests and the payback math |
| Attend, no booth | Passes audience tests, fails economics; meetings booked ahead |
| Speak only | A session is available; the audience is right; the floor is weak |
| Host alongside | A partner or customer anchors a dinner or workshop near the show |
| Pass | Fails the threshold or the lead time |
