This runs weekly. It produces the raw material so the founder's writing slot starts with choosing, not with staring at a blank page.

## Before you start

This skill works on its own. If the person has their own marketing brain (or any folder of context files about their company), read the files listed under `reads` in the header first and cite them for every fact. If they do not have one, do not look for one: ask for the two or three facts this play needs and go on. Never invent a fact; a blank is a question for the person.

Tools: this play speaks in verbs (check the CRM, pull the call, schedule the post). `TOOLS.md` at the root of this repo lists the tools recommended for each verb. The company's own `1-context/stack.md` wins when it exists. No tool is required; a spreadsheet and a mail client cover most of it.

## The loop

1. Read the context (see Before you start) and the pillars from `founder-content-rhythm`.
2. Pull the week's material: call transcripts in full (what customers said, what prospects objected to, what a competitor claimed), merged releases through `release-to-narrative`, the proof tracker's new rows, the event activity from `5-current/calendar.md`, industry news from the trade outlets in `1-context/stack.md`.
3. For each candidate: the one observation worth a post, the pillar it belongs to, the proof it rests on and its clearance status through `permission-check`, and a first line in the founder's voice.
4. Balance the set across the pillars and the week's calendar: a launch week leans product, an event week leans the segment, a quiet week leans point of view.
5. Mark the ones that need the founder's own story or opinion to work; those are prompts, not drafts.
6. Deliver five to seven ideas as a table. The founder picks; `social-post` drafts the chosen ones; `content-calendar` schedules.
7. Log which ideas were picked, so the next week's set avoids repeats and learns the founder's preferences.

## What good looks like

- The best operator hands the founder three ideas they would have written anyway and two they would not have thought of.
- The mediocre version hands over generic topics with no source and no proof.
- Good output is a founder who posts twice a week without the posts being a chore.

## Rules

- MUST read full transcripts when an idea comes from a call.
- MUST mark the proof status of every idea.
- NEVER draft a customer's words that are not cleared.
- NEVER publish; the founder publishes.
