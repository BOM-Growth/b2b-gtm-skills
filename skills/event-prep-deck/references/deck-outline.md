# Team prep deck outline

Read when building a prep deck. Every section names where its facts come from. Build in this order; do not add a section the outline lacks without a reason.

| Section | What goes in | Pull from |
|---|---|---|
| Know the audience | Show size, halls, our zone, who we want to find, badge colors or roles that identify buyers | Event row, show website |
| Qualifying visitors | The two or three quick qualifiers, the polite exit, the scan-anyway rule | icp.md |
| Ending the conversation | Book the demo on the spot, what to note on the scan | funnel.md, event-lead-ingest |
| Goals and KPIs | Set by the sales lead before the prep call: meetings, scans, demos | Events system of record (KPIs stay out of the knowledge base) |
| Logistics | Venue, booth, hall hours, setup and teardown, attire, giveaways, scanners | Events system of record |
| Team and booth roles | Shift grid with two in the booth at all times, who floats, contact list, arrivals and departures | Events system of record |
| Events and sessions | Keynotes, competitor sessions, evening events, which need registration | Show website |
| Quick facts | Company facts cleared for public use only | proof-rules.md, company boilerplate |
| Conversation guidelines | The conduct rules | event-prep-deck, voice.md |
| Answering prospect questions | Product, workflow, buying and competitor questions with approved answers | personas.md (objections), proof-rules.md, product pages |
| Product demo | Flow, what is live versus demo-only, what is not shown | Release record, proof-rules.md |
| Proof points | Cleared claims and approved quotes only | proof-rules.md, permissions tracker |
| Competition | The kinds of competition at a show and the direct competitors likely present | Competitor pages in the knowledge base |

## Deck rules

- No pricing figures.
- No unapproved customer names.
- Nothing from the not-cleared list.
- No superlatives that are not on the cleared claims list.
- Current product and company names only.
