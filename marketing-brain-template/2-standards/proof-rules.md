## Customer-stated numbers

A number a customer states about their own results is fine when credited to that customer. The company makes no general claim of its own from it. Light cleanup of spoken quotes is fine; the meaning stays.

## Attribution without permission

Attribute by role or company type only ("a mid-size [segment] company") unless the customer is on the approved list.

## Banned patterns

- "Not just X, but Y" reframing.
- Buzzword stacks: revolutionary, seamless, cutting-edge, transformative, game-changing, unlock, delve, dive deep.
- Scene-setting openers: "in today's fast-paced world", "in the ever-evolving landscape".
- Elevating the mundane: "it's not a product, it's a way of life".
- False rhetorical questions: "what if you could", "are you ready to".
- Em dashes used for drama; italics for emphasis.
- Slogan lines that no person would say out loud.
- Disclaimers like "no sales pitch".
- Claims about a customer's staffing or headcount without their approval.
