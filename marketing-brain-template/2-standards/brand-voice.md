# Brand voice
Status: draft

How the company writes, and how the founder writes when it is their name on the post. `brand-voice` and `founder-voice` read this file.

## Company voice

- Direct, grounded, specific. Numbers and names over adjectives.
- Write how people in [industry] talk. Short sentences. No slogans.
- Lead with the customer's result, not the product.
- A company post may say "I" occasionally when a named person is speaking.
- Third-party sources may be named when citing market data.
