# Marketing brain

One folder of plain text files about this business. Humans edit them. The AI reads them before doing any marketing work, and files new information into them. Every skill in the B2B GTM skills repo reads from here when it exists and writes its output back.

- 1-context/ who we are: company, ideal customer, personas, offers, competitors, stack (which tool holds which record)
- 2-standards/ how we sound and what we may claim: brand voice, terminology, proof and permission rules
- 3-strategy/ where we are going: goals, positioning, messaging, funnel definitions
- 4-playbooks/ how we do things: content, outreach, CRM setup, and any play you have made your own
- 5-current/ what is happening now: priorities, calendar and thresholds, decisions log
- sources/ raw material (transcripts, emails, notes), never edited

How to start: paste this folder into your AI assistant and say "draft every file from what you already know about my business, mark anything unsure with (check)". Then correct it, one folder at a time.

How to add to it: paste a transcript, email or note into your AI assistant and say "file this into my marketing brain".

Rules: folders 1 to 4 hold what stays true for months; folder 5 holds what changes weekly. Nothing dated goes in 1 to 3. Deal values and customer commercials never go in here. Nothing from internal meeting notes enters the brain unless someone who attended says so.
