# Events

Status: draft
Playbook 14 of 19. Stage: Distribution. Usually runs after the ICP and outbound sequence exist, before committing to an annual event calendar. Every skill in it works on its own.

## What it produces

Every event chosen against a number, worked as a pipeline play from six weeks before to 30 days after, and reported so the company knows whether to go again.

## Why it matters

Trade shows, conferences, dinners and webinars are where B2B buyers are in a buying frame of mind, and they are expensive. Without pre-booked meetings, same-hour capture and two-day follow-up, the money buys a stack of badges. With them, one event can fill a quarter's pipeline.

## Order of operations

1. Score candidate events on whether the buyers are there, whether decision makers attend, whether meetings can be had, and cost against expected pipeline. Set the goal in numbers.
2. Write the run sheet with roles and dates.
3. Filter the attendee list against the ICP and book a third of the meetings before arrival.
4. Prepare the stand or the meetings: the one-liner, the leave-behind, the three questions, the ask.
5. Capture every conversation the same hour with a fit label and a next step.
6. Follow up within two business days; hot leads next morning from the person they met.
7. Report at 30 days and decide: again, smaller, or skip.

## Who is involved

The go-to-market lead plans and reports. Whoever sells takes the meetings and follows up. Everyone on site captures. The founder decides on the number.

## How long it takes

Six weeks before to 30 days after. The on-site days are the smallest part.

## Signs it needs revisiting

- Badges in a pocket a week later.
- No meetings booked before arrival.
- No report at 30 days.

| Skill | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `events` | playbook | Trade shows, conferences, regional and trade association meetings, speaking slots, hosted dinners and webinars: "should we do this show", "what do we need for", "where are we at with the conference", "booth", "attendee list", "lead capture", "follow up from the show", "the association meeting", "speaking deadline". |
| `event-playbook` | play | Run a trade show, conference, dinner or webinar as a pipeline play: choose the event, book meetings before it starts, qualify and capture on site, follow up within days, and report what it produced in leads, meetings and pipeline. |
| `event-plan` | play | Once a show is a go and it is more than the minimum lead time out: "plan the show", "what do we need for", "where are we at with", "the checklist", "booth logistics", "who's going", "travel", "sponsorship deliverables". |
| `event-outreach` | play | In the outreach window before a show: "book meetings before the show", "who do we email about the show", "pre-show campaign", "the booth draw", "attendee list outreach", "tell customers we'll be there", "open deals at the show". |
| `event-prep-deck` | play | In the final prep before a show: "team prep deck", "prep call for the show", "talk tracks", "what do we say at the booth", "booth rules", "who's on which shift". |
| `event-lead-ingest` | play | On every show day and the day after: "process today's scans", "tier the leads", "who did we meet", "get the badge scans into the CRM", "end of day summary", "the scans are a mess". |
| `event-follow-up` | play | From the day after a show until every lead has an outcome: "follow up from the show", "where are the event leads", "has anyone contacted them", "is follow-up happening", "what happened to the scans". |
| `event-debrief` | play | In the week after a show and again at the payback horizon: "debrief the show", "how did it go", "what did the conference produce", "was it worth it", "lessons for next year". |
| `event-scorecard` | play | When deciding whether to exhibit at, attend, sponsor or speak at a show: "should we do this show", "is it worth it", "should we sponsor", "which shows next year", "an event is coming up soon". |
| `association-play` | play | For regional and trade associations: "join the association", "their meeting is next month", "get on their member list", "who runs the association", "speak to their members", "they invited us to speak", "we keep hearing about these meetings too late". |
| `webinar-play` | play | For events the company hosts: a webinar, a dinner near a show, a workshop, a customer session. |
| `speaking-proposal` | play | When a speaking slot is possible: "the call for speakers is open", "they asked us to present", "submit a session", "the deck for the talk", "co-present with a customer", "when is the deadline". |
| `event-follow-up-check` | loop | Daily through the follow-up window after any event: "where are we on the event leads", "did everyone get followed up", "the post-event check". |
