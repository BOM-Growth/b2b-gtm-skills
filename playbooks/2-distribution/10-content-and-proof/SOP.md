# Content and proof

Status: draft
Playbook 10 of 19. Stage: Distribution. Usually runs after positioning is written, before paid amplification. Every skill in it works on its own.

## What it produces

Two or three posts a week from the founder, one article a month, written from what is already being said on calls, with a ten-minute approval routine and a monthly check of what to make more of.

## Why it matters

Buyers check the founder's profile before they reply. Consistent, specific content in the founder's words is what makes a cold email warm and a demo easier. The first hundred pieces will be rough; consistency beats quality early. The raw material is already in the calls.

## Order of operations

1. Set the shape: one channel, two or three posts a week, one article a month.
2. Pick three content lanes and keep them for a quarter.
3. Mine the week's calls for questions, phrases, objections, results and decisions.
4. Draft the posts in the founder's voice; the idea grid fills gaps when calls run dry.
5. Approve in one doc per week; nothing goes out unapproved.
6. Schedule, post, reply to every comment the same day.
7. Recycle what worked after 60 to 90 days; review by lane monthly.

## Who is involved

The founder approves and replies. The go-to-market lead or the assistant drafts. Whoever runs sales flags the call moments worth a post.

## How long it takes

An hour a week to draft, ten minutes to approve, fifteen minutes a day to reply. Routine by week four.

## Signs it needs revisiting

- Posting has stopped for two weeks.
- Nothing in a month got a comment or a saved post.
- The lanes no longer match what buyers ask about.

## Skills in this playbook

| Skill | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `permission-check` | foundation | Before any customer name, logo, quote, number or story appears in a piece, and when someone asks "can we use X", "is this quote approved", "can we name them", "is that number cleared". |
| `content-proof` | playbook | The content engine and customer proof: "content calendar", "what are we posting", "post ideas", "founder post", "company page", "blog", "case study", "testimonial", "customer story", "video", "clip", "did you know", "the quote bank". |
| `endless-idea-generator` | play | Generate 100 social media or content post ideas from two short lists, pick the best three, and draft them. |
| `call-to-content` | play | Turn sales calls, customer calls, internal discussions and existing material into a steady stream of posts, articles and emails in the founder's voice, with a simple approval and posting routine. |
| `founder-content-rhythm` | play | Weekly for the founder's own content: "ideas for this week's posts", "what did the founder see this week", "prep the content meeting", "finalize the founder's drafts", "schedule next week's founder posts". |
| `content-calendar` | play | To plan or change what gets published: "what's on the calendar", "move that post", "add the launch posts", "we need something for the show", "fill next week", "what's the plan for the blog". |
| `social-post` | play | For company page posts: "draft a post about", "turn this into a post", "posts from this transcript", "a batch of posts for the launch", "event post", "customer post", "revise this post". |
| `schedule-post` | play | When approved posts need to go out: "schedule these", "post it Thursday", "queue next week's posts", "move the post to Friday", "is it scheduled". |
| `blog-edit` | play | To get a long-form piece ready to publish: "clean up this article", "fix the links", "add internal CTAs", "find real sources for these claims", "make it ready to publish", "polish the draft", "this was ghostwritten". |
| `product-tip-series` | play | For the recurring feature-tip series to customers and followers: "did you know post", "feature tip this week", "a short on the new tool", "customers don't know this exists", "what shipped that we haven't talked about". |
| `customer-video-cut` | play | When customer footage needs cutting: "cut the interview", "make clips from the testimonial", "a vertical version", "a looping video for a booth", "add captions", "the video has no sound". |
| `proof-capture` | play | The moment proof appears: a quote on a call, a video testimonial submitted, a number a customer shares, a positive line in an email, a review. |
| `case-study` | play | For a customer story from candidate to published: "write a case study", "customer story for", "a customer might do a story", "prep for the interview", "clip log", "co-marketing posts from the case study", "track the case study pipeline". |
| `founder-weekly-ideas` | loop | Every week before the founder's writing slot: "ideas for this week's posts", "what should I write about", "the founder content prompts", "what happened that's worth a post". |
