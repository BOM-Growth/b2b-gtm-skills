# Sales enablement

Status: draft
Playbook 7 of 17. Stage: Build. Usually runs after the offer is written, before hiring a salesperson. Every skill in it works on its own.

## What it produces

A discovery call that qualifies and learns, a demo that shows the buyer their own problem solved and ends with the ask, and one page a salesperson works from with every script, asset, price and template at the step where it is used. Plus the process for turning happy customers into testimonials and case studies.

## Why it matters

The founder knows how to sell this and nobody else does. Writing the call down, and putting every asset in one place, is what lets a new salesperson run a week without asking where anything is, and what makes every call produce the same notes.

## Order of operations

1. Define what a qualified buyer looks like in four or five facts.
2. Write the discovery structure, the question bank and the call notes template.
3. Write the demo script and the deck outline around the buyer's situation.
4. Build the command center: the process steps with every asset linked, prices, objections, competitors and rules.
5. List the gaps with owners and dates.
6. Set the testimonial and case study process.
7. Test the page with someone who has never sold the product; fix every question they ask.

## Who is involved

Whoever sells today supplies the process and the recordings. The go-to-market lead writes it down. A new hire tests it. The founder sets the pricing ranges.

## How long it takes

The scripts in a week. The command center in a week. Reviewed every two weeks against recorded calls for the first quarter, then monthly.

## Signs it needs revisiting

- Demos run long and end without a next step.
- Assets are being rebuilt or cannot be found.
- A new salesperson is starting.
