# ICP and wedge

Status: draft
Playbook 3 of 19. Stage: Foundation. Usually runs after discovery and diagnosis, before positioning and messaging. Every skill in it works on its own.

## What it produces

One page that says who the company sells to first, why that segment, who is in the room when they buy, and how to tell in five minutes whether a lead fits. Everything downstream is written for this one segment.

## Why it matters

A company that sells to everyone is hard to recommend, hard to find and hard to write for. A specific segment makes the message sharp, the lists buildable and the proof reusable. The segment is a public choice; the company can still take a good deal outside it. Expansion comes after the first segment works, not instead of it.

## Order of operations

1. Pull the evidence: current customers, won and lost deals, where inbound comes from, what customers say the problem is.
2. List three to six candidate segments, each specific enough to search for.
3. Score them on the seven questions in the skill. Pain must be clear; existing traction breaks ties.
4. Pick the wedge. Keep the runner-up as a backup, not a second target.
5. Write the ICP card, the buying committee and the qualification rules.
6. Check the segment is big enough for the revenue goal; widen geography before industry.
7. Set a 30 to 60 day test and a review date.

## Who is involved

The founder or whoever owns revenue decides. Whoever runs sales reviews the qualification rules before they are used. The assistant drafts and scores.

## How long it takes

One session of 60 to 90 minutes, plus a short second pass after sleeping on it. It is a best informed guess, corrected by real conversations.

## Signs it needs revisiting

- The company keeps winning deals outside the card.
- Three conversations in a row describe a different problem than the card says.
- The test date passes with fewer conversations than planned.

## Skills in this playbook

| Skill | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `icp-lists` | playbook | Anything about who we target: "review the ICP", "are we targeting the right accounts", "build a list for", "is this account a fit", "filter these leads", "tier my accounts", "who should we go after", "what's our TAM". |
| `icp-and-wedge` | play | Choose the first customer segment to go after (the wedge), define the ideal customer profile, map the buying committee and write qualification rules a person or an agent can apply. |
| `build-target-list` | play | When a play needs accounts or contacts: "build a list for the show", "who's in the association", "ad audience for", "this week's cohort", "target accounts in [region]", "everyone in the TAM who", "contacts at these companies". |
| `icp-fit-check` | play | When one account needs a verdict: "is this a fit", "should we take this demo", "tier this account", "is this company in our ICP", "they're small, do we still want them", "check them against the TAM". |
| `audience-icp-filter` | play | When a list already exists and someone needs to know who on it is worth contacting: event scans, webinar registrants, an association member list, a partner's leads, a newsletter export. |
