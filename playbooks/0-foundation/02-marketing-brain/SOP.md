# Marketing brain

Status: draft
Playbook 2 of 17. Stage: Foundation. Usually runs after discovery and diagnosis, or on day one. Every skill in it works on its own.

## What it produces

One folder of plain text files that holds everything about the company's go-to-market: who it is, who it sells to, what it sells, how it sounds, where it is going, how it does things, and what is happening now. People edit it. AI assistants read it before doing any work and file new information into it.

## Why it matters

Without it, every piece of marketing work starts with explaining the business again, and the output is generic. With it, a new hire or an AI assistant has day-one context, decisions carry their reasons, and nothing learned in a meeting is lost. Every other playbook reads from it and writes back to it when it exists.

## Order of operations

1. Copy the template folder into Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint or a repo.
2. Have the assistant draft every file from what it already knows; mark unknowns rather than inventing.
3. Correct the drafts one folder at a time, five minutes each.
4. After every meeting, paste the notes or transcript and say file this.
5. Once a month, read the foundation files and fix anything stale; move dated material to the current folder.

## Who is involved

The founder or the person who owns go-to-market owns the folder. Anyone on the team can file to it. AI assistants read and file; they never delete history.

## How long it takes

An afternoon to set up. Ten minutes after each meeting to keep current.

## Signs it needs revisiting

- The assistant is asking questions the files should answer.
- A file has not changed in three months while the business has.
- Two people describe the customer differently.

## Skills in this playbook

| Skill | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `gtm-context` | foundation | When a question or task touches the company's go-to-market facts: who we sell to, what we claim, how we sound, which tool holds which record, what the funnel stages mean, what the calendar allows. |
| `decision-log` | foundation | When a GTM decision gets settled in a chat, a call or a review: a naming rule, a claim retired, a reward amount, a hero line, a segment added, a channel paused. |
| `marketing-brain` | play | Set up and maintain a marketing brain, a folder of plain text files about a business that an AI assistant reads before doing any marketing work. |
| `daily-brain-sweep` | loop | Every morning: "the morning sweep", "what changed overnight", "update the brain", "anything new from the calls yesterday". |
